# Global Airbnb Analysis: Key Insights

Based on 279,712 listings, 182,024 hosts and 5,373,143 reviews across 10 cities (reviews dated Nov 2008 to Mar 2021).

## 1. A few cities dominate the market

| City | Share of listings | Share of reviews |
|---|---|---|
| Paris | 23.1% | 22.6% |
| New York | 13.2% | 15.8% |
| Sydney | 12.0% | 9.0% |
| Rome | 9.9% | 20.7% |
| Hong Kong | 2.5% | 2.5% |

- Paris, New York and Sydney hold **48.4% of listings**. Paris, Rome and New York account for **59.1% of reviews**.
- Rome stands out: it has under 10% of listings but over 20% of reviews, about 40 reviews per listing compared with about 19 in Paris.

## 2. Entire places are the main product

Entire places make up 65.1% of listings, private rooms 31.1%, hotel rooms 2.1% and shared rooms 1.7%.

## 3. Growth peaked in 2015, then COVID hit

- Listings by the year the host joined peaked in **2015 (44,886)**, dipped to 26,553 in 2017, and partly recovered to 31,447 in 2019.
- Reviews peaked in **2019 (1.63M)** and fell **about 54% in 2020 (755K)**, reflecting the collapse in travel.

## 4. Ratings are high everywhere, but vary by city

| City | Avg rating (out of 100) | Superhost share |
|---|---|---|
| Mexico City | 94.8 | 31.9% |
| Rio de Janeiro | 94.6 | 17.1% |
| Paris | 93.1 | 12.5% |
| Istanbul | 91.1 | 13.2% |
| Hong Kong | 89.7 | 18.1% |

- Mexico City and Rio rate highest; Hong Kong and Istanbul lowest.
- Cleanliness and value for money are the lowest-scoring categories in most cities.
- Superhosts (about 18% of listings) average **97.0** vs **92.3** for other hosts.

## 5. Seasonality differs by hemisphere

- **Paris and Rome:** reviews rise from late spring and peak from June to October.
- **New York:** stays high from September through December.
- **Sydney:** peaks in the southern summer (November to January).

## 6. Most guests review once

About **86% of reviewers wrote a single review** and about 99% wrote three or fewer. One reviewer has 283 reviews, which may be a data issue or a frequent traveller.

## 7. Hosting is partly professionalised

About 17.5% of hosts manage more than one listing, and the largest single host has 627.

## Limitations

- **Prices are in local currency**, so they cannot be compared across cities. Price analysis should stay within one city or use converted values.
- **Rating coverage is uneven.** Only 46% of Istanbul listings and 53% of Hong Kong listings have review scores, against 76% in Rome. Low-coverage cities may be less reliable.
- Reviews are a rough proxy for bookings, not a direct measure of them.
- Findings show patterns, not causes.
